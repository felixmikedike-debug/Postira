❓ How does this work?
  1. Pick a presenter – tap one of the faces under the picture.
  2. Add a voice – choose “Upload audio” and select an audio file from your phone or computer (a voice memo works great), or choose “Type what to say”.
  3. Press Preview to watch the result right here.
  4. Press Download video to save an MP4 you can post anywhere. Keep this page open until it finishes – it takes about as long as the video itself.
Tips: a clear audio file with little background noise gives the best lip movement. Audio you upload stays on your own device.

⚙️ Settings · only needed for “Type what to say”

“Type what to say” turns your words into speech using a voice service. If your provider gave you a web address (and maybe a key), paste them below. If you upload audio, you can ignore this.

For developers: what the service must returnThe page sends a POST request with JSON {"text", "length_scale", "gap_ms"} to the address above (the key is sent as a Bearer token). Reply with audio (wav or mp3), or with JSON containing url, audio_url or audio_base64.
